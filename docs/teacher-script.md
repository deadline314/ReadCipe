# 老師手寫講稿（逐字轉錄）

來源：`references/teacher-notes/card1…card8*.png`（老師做 PPT 時寫的講稿小卡），以及 `youtube-link-slide.png`。
這是簡報 F1 講稿的依據。刪除線表示老師劃掉的字；括號裡是老師的中文註記。

## 小卡 1（黃）：開場 → 簡報第 1 頁
1. Good afternoon, everyone. How are you?
   1. Today, we are going to learn the steps for making vanilla cupcakes.
   2. **★ We are not baking today.**（老師用橘框圈起來並打星號）
   3. Today, we will learn the ingredients and the steps.
   4. Next week, we will make the cupcakes together.

## 小卡 2（黃）：選杯子蛋糕 → 第 2 頁
2. Which cupcake do you like?
   1. Look at these cupcakes.
   2. Which one do you like?
   3. Number one, two, or three?
   4. Think for five seconds.
   5. Now, talk with your partner.
   6. Tell your partner which cupcake you like and why.
   - **★ You can use this sentence:**（橘框）
     "I like number __2__ because it is __cute__." ‹it is colourful›

## 小卡 3（綠）：剪下排序 → 第 3 頁
3. **Cut out and Sort the steps**（框起來）
   1. Now, it's your turn.
   2. Take out your worksheet.
   3. Cut out all the steps and pictures and ingredients.
   4. Please cut them out carefully.

## 小卡 4（綠）：做自己的食譜 → 第 4 頁
4. Make your own cupcake recipe（recipe 上方有發音提示「芮saepi」）
   1. Now, make your own cupcake recipe.
   2. **First**, take your coloured ~~card~~ sheet.
   3. **Then**, put the steps in the correct order
   4. **Next**, match each step with the correct picture.
   5. **Finally**, ~~glue~~ check the steps, pictures, and ingredients onto your coloured ~~card~~ sheet.
   - ⇨ You have three minutes. Ready? Let's start.

## 小卡 5（綠）：看影片對答案 → 第 5 頁
5. Great job!
   1. Now, let's watch a short video.
   2. Please watch carefully.
   3. Pay attention to the steps.
   4. Check your recipe.
   5. If the order is not correct, change it. Make your recipe correct.
   6. Ready? Let's watch!
   - （二倍速播放～）
   - （棕色補寫）You can discuss with your group members.
   - Is you(r) recipe correct now?
   - ~~Please glue everything onto your coloured sheet.~~（整句劃掉）

影片網址（`youtube-link-slide.png`）：https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dGgE68DBvkw

## 小卡 6（綠）：食材 → 第 6 頁
6. Now, let's look at the ingredients.
   1. We need these ingredients to make vanilla cupcakes.
   2. Let's read them together.
   - （～帶讀食材～）
   3. These are the ingredients for our cupcakes.
   - ⇨ Great job! Let's move on to the next step.

## 小卡 7（綠）：步驟一 → 第 8 頁（Step 2–6 依同句型延伸）
7. Now let's look at step one.
   1. Group 1, please read Step 1 together（學生念）
   2. Great job!
   3. Now, let's check the details together
      - First, mix the milk and honey.
      - Then, heat it and let it cool.
   4. You can write some notes on your coloured sheet.

## 小卡 8（綠）：結尾 → 第 16 頁
8. Great job, everyone.
   1. Today you learned the ingredients and the steps for making vanilla cupcakes
   2. You all did a great job.
   3. Give yourselves a big hand.
   4. Next week, we will bake vanilla cupcakes together.
   5. Thank you, everyone. See you next week.
