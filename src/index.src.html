AI script. Step one: prepare the liquids. We mix milk and honey. Then we heat them in the microwave for thirty seconds, and let it cool.

Let's try it. Add the milk… add the honey… now press Heat. The microwave is on! Thirty seconds… ding! Stir it well, then let it cool down.

AI script. Step two: prepare the dry ingredients. Put the cake flour, the baking powder and the salt in the sieve.

Now shake, shake, shake! Sieving makes the flour light and fluffy, with no lumps.

Ask: What three dry ingredients do we sieve? (cake flour, baking powder, salt)

Students can call out “Shake!” each time you click.

Whisk the eggs and sugar

Whisk eggs on high speed for 2 minutes. Add sugar and mix until creamy.

AI script. Step three: whisk the eggs and sugar. Crack three eggs into the bowl. Whisk on high speed for two minutes. Bzzzz!

Now add the sugar, one hundred ten grams, and whisk again until it is pale and creamy.

Ask: What color is the mixture now? (pale yellow) How does it feel? (creamy, fluffy)

AI script. Step four, part one: add the sunflower seed oil. Pour it slowly, from the side of the bowl.

Hold the button to pour. Keep the slider on slow. Oh no, too fast! Slowly, slowly… one hundred twenty grams. Now whisk for another three minutes.

Ask: Do we pour the oil fast or slow? (slowly) Where do we pour it? (from the side)

Add flavor

AI script. Step four, part two: now the flavor! Add one teaspoon of vanilla extract. Smell it, mmm! Then add the honey milk from step one.

Now mix with a spatula. Drag around the bowl, round and round, until everything is the same color.

Ask: Which liquid did we make in step one? (honey milk) What gives the cupcake its flavor? (vanilla)

AI script. Step five: mix with flour. Don't add all the flour at once! Add it in three or four parts.

Add some flour, then mix gently with the spatula. Drag slowly around the bowl until the white flour disappears. Then add more.

Ask: Do we mix fast or gently? (gently) How many times do we add flour? (three or four)

STEP 6
Fill the cups & Bake

Spoon the batter into cupcake cases.
Bake at 180°C for 16 minutes.

AI script. Step six: fill the cups and bake. Use a spoon to put the batter into the cupcake cases. Fill each case about two-thirds full.

Click each case to fill it. Twelve cases! Now bake at one hundred eighty degrees for sixteen minutes. Look, the cupcakes are rising!

Ask: How hot is the oven? (180 degrees) How long do we bake? (16 minutes)
