    Now, let's look at the ingredients.

    We need these ingredients to make vanilla cupcakes. Let's read them together. (Click “Read together”: each card lights up in turn.)

    Cake flour, 190 grams. Baking powder, one and a half teaspoons. Salt, a quarter teaspoon. Sunflower oil, 120 grams. Sugar, 110 grams. Eggs, three. Vanilla extract, one teaspoon. Milk, 40 grams. Honey, 30 grams.

    These are the ingredients for our cupcakes.
